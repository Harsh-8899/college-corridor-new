About Us

About College Corridor

College Corridor is an education consultancy and admissions advisory platform helping students navigate premium college and university admissions across India.

We work with students targeting leading institutions for B.Tech, MBBS, MBA, Law and other professional programs, providing personalized guidance based on academic profile, entrance performance, budget and career goals.

From college discovery and comparison to counselling, choice filling and final admission assistance, we help students make informed decisions at every stage.

Our Story

College Corridor was founded to bring transparent, data-driven strategy to college admissions across India. We bridge the gap between entrance exam scores and seat allotment by helping students evaluate realistic options and make informed decisions.
