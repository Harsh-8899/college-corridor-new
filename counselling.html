Pitfall Avoidance

THE BIGGEST ADMISSION MISTAKE ISN'T ALWAYS A LOW SCORE.

Common Mistakes Made Every Admission Season:

❌ Choosing too quickly under pressure or last-day panic.
❌ Choosing too conservatively and missing out on better target colleges.
❌ Filling choices randomly without understanding round upgrade rules.
❌ Ignoring key counselling pathways (e.g. open state quotas, central quotas).
❌ Focusing only on brand name while ignoring fees, location, clinical exposure, placements, or long-term growth.
❌ Simply not knowing what you don't know.

You don't need more random information. You need a strategy built around your profile.

Risk Profile Alignment

YOUR COUNSELLING STRATEGY SHOULD MATCH YOUR RISK PROFILE.

Not every student should fill choices the same way.

🔴 AGGRESSIVE

"I want to maximize my upside."

Higher-risk choices designed to pursue stronger opportunities. Best suited for students comfortable with uncertainty.

🟡 BALANCED

"I want ambition + security."

A carefully structured combination of ambitious, realistic and safer options. The preferred approach for many students.

🟢 SAFE

"My priority is securing a seat."

A more conservative strategy focused on reducing admission uncertainty and securing a verified option early.

Which one is right for you?

There is no universal answer. It depends on: Your rank + category + domicile + budget + preferences + eligibility + available seats + risk tolerance.
