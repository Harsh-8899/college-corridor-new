Evaluating Engineering Disciplines

CSE & IT
Computer Science & Engineering

High current placement demand, fast industry evolution. Requires strong algorithmic fundamentals and continuous self-learning.

AI & DATA
AI, ML & Data Science

Specialised software track. We evaluate curriculum depth vs core CSE to avoid superficial rebrandings by private universities.

ECE
Electronics & Communication

Versatile engineering branch bridging hardware, semiconductor VLSI design, robotics, and software careers.

EEE / EE
Electrical Engineering

Rigorous core discipline with strong opportunities in EV technology, power systems, PSU GATE jobs, and higher studies.

MECH & AERO
Mechanical & Aerospace

Core manufacturing, automotive, and defence engineering. Best pursued at institutions with established laboratory infrastructure.

CIVIL & OTHER
Civil, Chemical & Biotech

Specialised core fields offering distinct public sector, research, global master's, and niche corporate career paths.
