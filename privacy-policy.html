Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out lead forms, request counselling sessions, or interact with College Corridor, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Details: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address.
  • Academic Profile: Program of Interest, Examination Scores/Ranks (NEET, JEE, CAT, CLAT, etc.), 10th/12th percentages, Category, Domicile state.
  • Preferences: Preferred budget, target locations, course preferences, and specific enquiry messages.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and site interaction data via cookies and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate education consultancy and advisory purposes:

  • To contact you and provide requested counselling, college prediction, and admission guidance services.
  • To evaluate eligibility for specific courses, quotas, state counselling, and institutions.
  • To send updates on exam schedules, counselling deadlines, cutoff changes, and relevant educational opportunities.
  • To improve our website functionality, service quality, and user experience.

3. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the counselling purpose for which it was collected, or to comply with applicable legal and administrative obligations. If you wish to have your data removed from our records, you may submit a request to us.

4. Third-Party Sharing

College Corridor does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared with authorized educational institutions or partner counselling service providers when explicitly requested by you or required to facilitate your admission guidance process.

5. Security & User Rights

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by College Corridor by contacting us at our official email address.

6. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us via our Contact Page or write to admissions@collegecorridor.com.
