Common Questions

NEET & MBBS Counselling FAQs

What is the difference between MCC All India Quota (AIQ) and State Quota NEET counselling?

All India Quota (AIQ) covers 15% of seats in government medical colleges across India plus 100% of seats in Central Universities, AIIMS, JIPMER, and Deemed Universities, conducted centrally by the MCC. State Quota counselling covers the remaining 85% of seats in state government medical colleges and state private institutions, administered by respective state counselling authorities.

How does choice filling and choice locking strategy affect MBBS seat allotment?

Choice filling determines the exact sequence in which the counselling algorithm evaluates your preferences against available seats. A structured choice list balances aspirational, realistic, and safe options to prevent seat loss or locking into less preferred colleges in earlier rounds.

What happens during the Stray Vacancy & Mop-Up rounds in NEET counselling?

The Mop-Up and Stray Vacancy rounds fill seats left vacant after Round 1 and Round 2. Rules vary between MCC AIQ and State Authorities — candidates must carefully evaluate security deposit forfeit rules, eligibility criteria, and offline vs online allotment procedures.

Does College Corridor provide guidance for PG Medical (MD / MS) admissions?

Yes. College Corridor provides end-to-end guidance for NEET-PG and INI-CET counselling — assisting doctor candidates in rank-wise specialty selection (General Medicine, Radiodiagnosis, Paediatrics, Orthopaedics, Surgery), clinical exposure evaluation, and fee analysis across government, private, and deemed institutes.
