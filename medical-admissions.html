Don't Just Register. Build a Strategy.

MCC Counselling — Don't Enter Without a Plan.

MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) manages All India Quota (15%), Deemed Universities (100%), Central Universities (AIIMS, JIPMER, BHU, AMU), and ESIC quota seats.

Every round in MCC has unique rules regarding seat allotment, free exit, security deposit forfeiture, and eligibility for state rounds. We help you build a personalized MCC strategy around your exact score and category rank.

Borderline / Low Score Scenario

"My Score Isn't Very High. Can Counselling Still Help Me?"

Answer: Counselling doesn't change your score. It changes how intelligently you use the options available to you.

A borderline rank requires a data-backed search across open state private colleges, deemed universities, management quotas, and fee structures to find genuine opportunities that others overlook.

Top Rank / High Score Scenario

"I Have a Good Rank. Why Do I Need Counselling?"

Answer: Because a good rank gives you more choices, not automatically the right decision.

With more options come complex decisions: AIIMS vs top state government college, patient load comparisons, PG quota benefits, internal reservation, and location preferences.

The Cost of a Poor Decision Can Be Much Higher Than the Cost of Good Guidance.

Common Consequences of Unguided Choice Filling:

  • ❌ Missing out on a superior government or private college due to flawed preference ordering.

Good guidance is not about guaranteeing an impossible outcome. It's about improving the quality of the decisions you make when they matter most.
